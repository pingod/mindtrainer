    Frequently Asked Questions

    Which mode should I choose?

    Choose 30 Targets to measure average time per target, 30 Seconds to count stationary hits, or Moving 30s to practice tracking.

    How is my score calculated?

    30 Targets shows the average time for each successful hit, where lower is faster. The two 30-second modes count successful hits, where higher is better.

    What counts as a miss?

    In 30 Targets, missed clicks do not count and the timer keeps running. In 30 Seconds, a missed click or expired target costs one life. Targets expire after 3, 2.2, or 1.5 seconds as the run progresses. In Moving 30s, only a missed click costs a life.

    Why does my score change between devices?

    Screen size, pointer sensitivity, refresh rate, browser performance, and input delay can all affect it.

    Does sound affect my result?

    No. Timing is recorded before optional sound and visual feedback are played.

    Where are my results saved?

    Each mode keeps its 20 most recent results in this browser, including runs that end when all lives are lost. In the timed modes, only full 30-second runs can set a personal best. Interrupted runs are not saved.
