Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Short version

PickTests does not require an account. Test results may be stored locally in your browser. If enabled, Google Analytics may help us understand site usage, and Google AdSense may display ads.

Information We Handle

PickTests does not ask you to provide your name, phone number, address, or payment information to use the tests.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and message. Our hosting and security providers may process limited technical information such as your IP address, browser, device, pages visited, approximate location, and visit time. If analytics or advertising services are enabled, those providers may process similar information.

Test Results and Local Storage

Test results, recent activity, or preferences may be stored in your browser using local storage. This information stays on your device unless a feature clearly states otherwise. You can remove it by clearing PickTests site data in your browser.

Google Analytics and AdSense

If enabled, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers to measure traffic and understand how PickTests is used.

If ads are displayed, Google AdSense and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize, limit repetition, and measure ads.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate and protect PickTests, improve the tests, understand usage, display and measure ads, respond to messages, and comply with legal obligations. It may be processed by service providers that support these purposes.

Email messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed. Browser-stored test data remains on your device until you clear it. Third-party providers keep information according to their own policies and settings.

Your Choices

You can block or clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings and manage Google ad personalization through Google Ads Settings.

Where required by law, PickTests will display a consent message before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

Children’s Privacy

PickTests is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when PickTests or the services it uses change. The latest version will appear on this page.
