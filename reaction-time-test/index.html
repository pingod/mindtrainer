Frequently Asked Questions

How do I take the test?

Choose a mode and start the test. Wait while the area is orange, then respond as soon as it turns green.

What happens if I respond too soon?

The round is marked “Too soon!” and no score is recorded. You can retry the same round immediately.

Which mode should I choose?

Use 1 Round for a quick check. Choose 5 Rounds when you want an average and a better view of variation.

What is a good reaction time?

There is no single score that fits everyone. Your most useful comparison is with your own results on the same device.

Why do my results change between devices?

Screen refresh rate, browser performance, and input delay can all affect the recorded time.

Where are my results saved?

Results are saved only in this browser’s local storage. Recent Tests can be cleared from this page.
